About MatRox Residence

Bringing the world's most sophisticated living standards to the heart of Cameroon.

Our Story

MatRox Residence was born from a singular vision: to bridge the gap between global hospitality excellence and the vibrant potential of Cameroon. We noticed that while the demand for high-end living was growing, the execution often fell short of international standards.

Founded by a consortium of local visionaries and international architects, we embarked on a mission to create spaces that aren't just buildings, but experiences. Every stone laid and every service curated at MatRox is a testament to our commitment to luxury without compromise.
